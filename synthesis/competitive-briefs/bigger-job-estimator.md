# Competitive Brief: Bigger Job Estimator
**Date:** June 2026 | **Focus:** Online boiler quote & estimation — install, service, repair

---

## Context

JustFix is building a conversational triage flow that quotes bigger boiler jobs (install, service, repair). The strategic bet: shift from unprofitable small jobs to higher-value work where CAC is justified. The competitive bar set in the PRD is Boxt — "fixed-price quote in 60 seconds, no home visit."

This brief assesses whether that bar is accurate, who else is in the space, and where JustFix has genuine room to differentiate.

---

## Competitor Deep Dives

### Boxt

**Positioning:** "Fixed-price, next-day boiler installation — no sales visits, no surprises."

**Quote flow:**
1. Answer questions about home, existing boiler, hot water use (~2 min)
2. Upload photos of current boiler, flue, pipework
3. Receive fixed-price quote immediately (includes boiler, flue, install, disposal, system flush)
4. Choose a date; pay upfront; next-day install available in most areas

**Pricing:** From £1,790 for combi-to-combi swap. Mid-range (3-bed home) typically £2,000–£2,600. Price match guarantee: beat any like-for-like quote by £50.

**Finance:** 0% APR over 12/24 months; longer terms at 11.9% APR.

**Service:** £8.99/mo subscription (£11.99 for landlords). Annual Gas Safe engineer visit to HHIC checklist.

**Repair:** Not an instant-quote product. Engineer dispatched to diagnose; unlimited repairs under the BOXT Life plan (£33.35/mo, covers install + annual service + unlimited repair/replacement).

**Trustpilot:** 4.8/5 from 59,000+ reviews. Strong on speed, professionalism, cleanliness.

**Weaknesses (from reviews):**
- Post-install customer service can be slow (leaks taking 5+ weeks to resolve)
- Complex or non-standard homes may see extras not in the initial fixed price
- Limited brand choice: primarily Worcester Bosch and Ideal
- Online-only photo assessment — some customers prefer a physical survey
- Repair response time inconsistency reported when on subscription plan

---

### iHeat

**Positioning:** Fast, no-nonsense fixed price — quote in ~40 seconds, no personal details required upfront.

**Quote flow:** Choose home size and heating setup → instant price shown before giving any contact details. Arguably the clearest UX in the market for installation quotes.

**Pricing:** Comparable to Boxt; competes on Viessmann and Worcester Bosch models.

**Strengths:** Fastest quote flow tested; live chat support during quote; very low friction before showing price.

**Weaknesses:** Primary focus is installation replacement; less known for service/repair.

---

### Heatable

**Positioning:** Honest, no-jargon boiler quotes that undercut British Gas.

**Quote flow:** Simple quiz (<90 sec) — current boiler, home size, hot water use. Visual, clean. Slightly intrusive about collecting contact info early.

**Pricing:** Often cheaper than British Gas; Viessmann and Worcester Bosch focus.

**Strengths:** Good UX, strong SEO presence, honest price framing.

**Weaknesses:** Occasionally prompts for contact info before showing price; narrower brand range.

---

### British Gas

**Positioning:** Trusted national brand with full heating lifecycle — install, service, repair, and cover.

**Quote flow:** Online quote tool available but slower; home survey option for complex jobs. More manual than Boxt/iHeat.

**Pricing:** Like-for-like combi swap from ~£2,578 (online) or £2,964 (post-survey). Typically £500–£1,000 more expensive than Boxt.

**Strengths:** Brand trust, especially with older homeowners. HomeCare plans are deeply embedded (millions of customers). Can handle complex/unusual installs.

**Weaknesses:** Slower, more expensive, less digital-native. High-pressure sales tactics noted by some customers.

## Positioning Analysis

**How competitors claim the space:**

- **Boxt:** "Fixed price, next day" — speed and certainty for installation
- **iHeat:** "Instant price, no faff" — radical simplicity, lowest-friction quote
- **Heatable:** "Honest price, cheaper than British Gas" — trust and value
- **British Gas:** "The name you trust for everything boiler-related" — broad lifecycle, brand safety

**What nobody owns:**

> *"A quote for any boiler job — repair, service, or install — in under 90 seconds, from a fixer who'll actually show up."*

The entire online fixed-price category is focused on **installation only**. Repair and service are either subscription-gated (Boxt Life) or call-to-book (British Gas). No one offers an instant, conversational quote for a boiler **repair** or a one-off **service**.

---

## Opportunities for JustFix

**1. Own the repair and service quote gap**
Every competitor either ignores repair quotes or hides them behind a subscription. A customer whose boiler has broken down today can't get an instant price anywhere online — they have to call or wait for a diagnosis visit. JustFix can own this moment.

**2. Conversational flow > form-filling**
The leading competitors use good questionnaire flows, but they're still form-based. A genuinely conversational triage (icon-tap → natural language) would feel materially different on mobile — especially for a stressed homeowner with a broken boiler.

**3. "Subject-to-survey" framing is a feature, not a bug**
Boxt's photo-upload model sometimes fails on complex jobs and creates post-booking friction. A flow that explicitly offers "firm quote" vs "subject-to-survey" is more honest and arguably builds more trust for complex jobs.

**4. PDF quote as differentiator**
No competitor offers a branded, downloadable quote document. For a larger job (£2k+ install), customers may want something to review and share with a partner. This also creates an organic sharing/referral surface.

**5. Fixer network as trust signal**
Boxt's nationwide model means customers get an unknown engineer. JustFix can position vetted, local fixers as a differentiator — especially for repair, where trust matters more than speed.

---

## Threats

**1. Boxt expanding into repair/service quotes**
Boxt already has a repair/service subscription (BOXT Life). Adding an instant repair quote to their flow is a natural next move. They have the brand, the SEO, and the fixer network to execute it quickly.

**2. iHeat's UX sets a very high bar**
40-second quote with no personal details upfront is a strong baseline. JustFix's flow needs to feel at least as fast and low-friction.

**3. Boxt's SEO dominance**
Boxt dominates organic search for "online boiler quote" and related terms. JustFix will need to rely on paid acquisition or existing customer base initially — organic won't be fast.

**4. Trust gap for an unfamiliar brand**
Customers booking a £2,000+ job from a name they don't recognise will need strong social proof. Review count/rating will matter at the quote page.

---

## Strategic Implications

**1. The PRD bar ("Boxt in 60 seconds") needs updating**
iHeat now delivers a fixed install quote in ~40 seconds with no personal details upfront. That's the actual bar for speed. JustFix needs to match or beat this.

**2. Repair is the genuinely underserved moment**
Everyone is chasing the installation replacement market. The customer whose boiler failed this morning — who needs a price and a fixer today — has nowhere to get an instant quote. This is JustFix's highest-differentiation opportunity, and it maps directly to the existing platform strengths (fixer dispatch, same-day availability).

**3. Service quoting is table stakes, not a differentiator**
Boxt already has this as a subscription. Position annual service as the upsell after a repair, not a standalone product.

**4. Prioritise the repair path in MVP alongside install**
The PRD currently lists repair as one of three install paths. Consider whether the repair quote flow should be treated as a first-class, separately validated journey — it's a different customer moment (urgent, stressed, not shopping around) and requires different question logic.

**5. The PDF quote is low-effort, high-signal**
No competitor has it. It should ship in MVP.

**6. Watch Boxt's BOXT Life expansion**
If they add an instant repair quote to their subscription acquisition funnel, the window for JustFix to own this narrows significantly. This is a reason to move on the repair path quickly.
