# Initiatives

_Last updated: 2026-06-04_

Initiatives represent strategic bets — the goals JustFix is working toward. Each initiative has one or more projects beneath it that deliver against the goal. This document is the strategic layer; see `roadmap.md` for what's actively in progress.

---

## Customer experience

### Turn the customer app into a competitive advantage
The current consumer-facing apps are functional but not differentiated. The goal is to redesign and rebuild the customer app so the experience itself becomes a reason to book, return, and recommend JustFix.

**Projects:** Redesign and rebuild consumer apps, Mobile app enhancements

---

### Book a job from a photo or video
Let customers show JustFix the problem directly — point their camera at it, get an AI-generated diagnosis and estimate, and book all in one flow.

**Projects:** Live video + MCP to estimate and book, Video triage, AI Diagnostic tool

---

### Book through any AI-powered channel
Let customers book through conversational AI interfaces — chat, messaging, or agent-based flows — wherever they are. Distinct from the native app; this is about meeting customers in the interfaces they already use.

**Projects:** Chat GPT App MVP

---

### Convert customers to subscribers
JustFix's subscription has a cold sales problem. The opportunity is to sell at the moment a customer already has a problem and has chosen JustFix — the warmest possible moment to make the offer.

**Projects:** BNPL partner model

---

## Fixer supply

### Secure a committed core fixer workforce
Move beyond fully ad-hoc matching by building a committed core of fixers engaged on a day rate. These fixers get first priority in matching, de-risking big jobs and improving quality consistency.

**Projects:** Day rate fixers

---

### Always send the best fixer
Ensure the best available fixer is always matched to a job — prioritising directly employed fixers first, then using ratings data to surface the most reliable self-employed fixers.

**Projects:** Rate fixers, Improve matching algorithm

---

### Improve the fixer experience
Reduce friction for fixers at every stage — from job management to completing work and getting paid — so that working with JustFix is easier than the alternative.

**Projects:** Voice agent - post appointment feedback, Migrate fixer repo and management from HubSpot to admin

---

## Operations & platform

### Manage all comms from one place
All customer and fixer messaging should run through JustFix, creating a complete record in the admin system and enabling ops to manage any booking without context-switching.

**Projects:** Customer WhatsApp Communications, WhatsApp fixer flow MVP, Whatsapp fixer flow P2

---

### Build the data foundation for big jobs
Introduce a formal "job" concept as the parent entity above bookings and appointments. Foundational infrastructure that unlocks big job quoting, multi-trade work, and better unit economics.

**Projects:** Introduce jobs above bookings, Bigger Job Estimator, Booking Estimator MCP

---

### Zero-touch fixer payments
Replace the manual fixer payment process with self-billing — JustFix generates the invoice on the fixer's behalf and triggers payment automatically once a job is confirmed complete.

**Projects:** Self-billing for fixers

---

### Run leaner without losing capability
Identify like-for-like tool and infrastructure replacements that reduce our cost base without compromising capability. Current targets: payment processing (Stripe → Ryft) and CRM (HubSpot → Go High Level).

**Projects:** Migrate payments to Ryft, Migrate to Go High Level, Migrate fixer repo and management from HubSpot to admin
